Life Reset — Scroll Se Goal Tak is engineered with a strict privacy-by-design, offline-first architecture:

  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.
  • No Internet Permission: The application does not request network access (android.permission.INTERNET is completely absent).
  • On-Device Only: All goals, habits, focus records, and recreational screen-time logs are stored exclusively in your device's private SQLite database.
  • No Accounts or Tracking: No registration, no advertising frameworks, and no third-party telemetry SDKs.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Life Reset — Scroll Se Goal Tak ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information.

We believe that personal productivity, habit cultivation, and screen-time reflections are deeply private matters. Life Reset does not require you to create an account, log in with an email, or connect to remote servers. The App operates independently on your physical Android device without external communications.

3. Information Handled Strictly on Your Device

All data processed by the App is created directly by you and never leaves your Android device:

A. Goals & Priorities

Goal titles, optional descriptions, target dates, completion timestamps, and designations for your daily top-3 priorities.

B. Habits & Streaks

Habit names, descriptions, icon identifiers, color selections, and calendar completion checkmarks used to calculate consistency streaks.

C. Focus Timer Sessions

Planned focus durations, actual elapsed seconds, optional linked goal references, session labels, and start/finish timestamps.

D. Recreational Screen-Time Logs

Manual estimates you enter for time spent on social media apps, along with calendar dates and personal notes. (Note: The App does not monitor your device usage automatically or inspect background apps; all entries are created manually by you).

E. Preferences & Session Recovery

Your daily recreational screen-time target (minutes), default focus timer interval, and temporary state timestamps used to resume an active timer if you switch apps.

4. Zero External Transmission & Permissions

  • No Network Permissions: The compiled release build does not contain the android.permission.INTERNET permission. The Android operating system physically prevents the App from making network connections.
  • No Third-Party SDKs: We do not embed analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob), or crash-reporting services (such as Sentry or Crashlytics).
  • No Hardware Tracking: The App does not access device sensors, cameras, microphones, contacts, or external storage.

5. Storage & Device Security

All information is kept strictly within Android’s secure application sandbox at /data/data/com.life.reset.app/:

  • Structured records are stored in a local SQLite database (life_reset.db).
  • Configuration limits and timer recovery data are stored via Android's local SharedPreferences.

Under Android's sandboxing security model, other apps installed on your device cannot access Life Reset's internal files.

6. Data Portability & JSON Export

You have full ownership of your data. The App features a built-in export tool accessible under Settings & Privacy > Export All Data (JSON):

  • Compiles all your local records (goals, habits, completions, focus logs, screen-time records, and preferences) into an open, human-readable JSON format.
  • Provides a "Copy to Clipboard" button so you can paste and store your personal backup wherever you choose.
  • Privacy Recommendation: When copying data to the system clipboard, ensure you do not leave sensitive text in the clipboard if you have other third-party apps with clipboard monitoring permissions.

7. Data Deletion Rights

You have continuous and complete control over your data lifecycle:

  1. In-App Reset: Go to Settings & Privacy > Reset & Clear > Delete All App Data. After confirming through two safety dialogs, the App atomically clears all records across all database tables and resets preferences.
  2. Operating System Clear Storage: You can clear all data at the system level via your phone's Settings > Apps > Life Reset > Storage > Clear Storage.
  3. App Uninstallation: Uninstalling Life Reset immediately erases the App’s private sandbox directory. No residual files or traces remain on your device.

8. Operating System Cloud Backups

If you have enabled Android's system-level device backup (such as Google Drive phone backup) in your device's operating system settings, Android may include the App’s internal sandbox database in your private, encrypted personal device backup. This is an operating system feature managed by Google and your personal Google account settings, not an in-app data transmission.

9. Children’s Privacy

Life Reset is a general audience self-improvement application. It does not solicit, gather, or store personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the App fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international child data privacy regulations.
